Skywa International School

UDISE Code: 10281705904

Run by Maa Urmila Welfare Trust

Skywa International School, established on April 07, 2013, is a premier English Medium co-educational facility nestled in Bakhtiyarpur, Patna (Bihar). With a strong commitment to nurturing young minds, the school offers a holistic learning experience for students from play school to upper primary levels. Located on Naya Tola Madhopur, bakhtiyarpur, Our school embraces a dynamic education strategy that empowers pupil to reach their full potential. Its dedicated staff and innovative programs create an atmosphere where every child can thrive, ensuring a well-rounded education for further generations.

  The school runs on the rules, regulations and co-educational curriculum based of CBSE. This school is divided into three group first Pre-Primary (Playgroup to UKG) Second Primary Section (Class–I to V) & Upper-Primary Section (Class-VI to VIII) sections are run under by versed and Efficient, Faculty Team.

Teaching Level

At present the school runs classes from Play-Group to Class VIII level.
