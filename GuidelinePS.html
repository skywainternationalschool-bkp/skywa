CODE OF CONDUCT FOR STUDENTS IN Skywa International School

The vision and mission of Skywa International School is to produce physically mentally and spiritually sound pupils. The students must inculcate some precious qualities and integrity. Every student of Daffodils Public School is instructed to follow the following rules of the school religiously.

1.   Presence in school with neat , clean and well-maintained school uniform is mandatory.
2.   Students must wear the school identity card otherwise they might not be allowed to enter the school premises.
3.   Every Student must be punctual in attending all classes, assemblies and activities.
4.   Students are expected to converse only in English while on school premises.However, this rule is not applicable during Hindi and Sanskrit periods.
5.   An ideal student of Daffodils Public School is Expected to nurture some valuable qualities like gentleness , Politeness, honest and integrity. They must be courteous too. Words like ' thank you', 'Sorry', 'Please' and 'Excuse me' must be made a part of his/her everyday vocabulary.
6.   The great Indian tradition of showing respect to parents, teachers and elders must be observed. Students are expected to stand quietly when a teacher/visitor enters the classroom and sit down only when told to do so. Students must inculcate the culture of greeting the principal, teachers and visitors respectfully.
7.   Any act of violence including physical or sexual abuse bullying or ragging is strictly prohibited C.B.S.E circular no. 24 dated 4th July 2009 will be strictly adhered to. Any act of violation of the norm will be severely dealt with.
8.   Students are instructed to :-

a.   Attend morning assemblies and stand quietly and respectfully during prayer and the singing of the National Anthem.
b.   Take complete responsibility and proper care of books, notebooks and all other belongings.
c.   Handle school property and equipment with care , remembering to return and replace them after use.
d.   Always be truthful at home , in school and on the playground.
e   .Never loiter aimlessly along the corridors, run about or shout while in the school.
f.   Strive to attain excellence in every aspect of your life whether academically socially or morally.
g.   Practice a Positive attitude, accepting defeat with a smile in a true sportsmen like spirit.
h.   Keep your surroundings clean and be environmentally conscious, remembering to use garbage receptacles.
i.   Never leave the school during school hours under any circumstance unless the Principal has granted permission to you.
j.   Bring your school diary everyday with you to school.
